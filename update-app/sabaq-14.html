يَاءُ الْمُتَكَلِّمSabaq 14 · pp. 31–32

Yaa-e Mutakallim — Possessive Ya (ي)

Is sabaq mein aap seekhenge ke kaise kisi bhi ism (noun) ke aakhir mein ي laga kar usse "mera/meri" bana sakte hain. Quran Kareem mein yeh pattern bohot zyada aata hai.

Kisi bhi ism (noun) ke aakhir mein Yaa-e Mutakallim (ي) laga dein to uska matlab hoga "mera" ya "meri". Jaise: بَيْتُ (ghar) + ي = بَيْتِي (mera ghar)

قَوَاعِدGrammar Note

Zaruri Baat: Arabi zuban mein yeh aam baat hai ke baaz auqaat ikhtisaar ke liye lafz ke aakhir se (ي) — yani "mera" ya "meri" ke mafhoom wali ya — ko hazf kar diya jata hai. Aur lafz ke aakhir mein sirf zer (kasra) baqi rehti hai. Jaise: رَبِّي se رَبِّ ban jaata hai.
