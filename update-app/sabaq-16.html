حُرُوفٌ مُتَّصِلَةٌ بِالْيَاءSabaq 16 · pp. 35–37

Huruf ke Saath Yaa-e Mutakallim (ي)

Is sabaq mein aap seekhenge ke jab (ي) yaani "mera / meri / mujh" ka lafz kisi Harf (particle) ke aakhir mein aata hai, toh woh kaisa dikhta hai aur uska kya matlab hota hai.

Huruf (particles) ke aakhir mein (ي) aane se 'mera / meri / mujh' ka matlab paida hota hai. Jaise: إِنَّ (Beshak) + ي = إِنِّي (Beshak main).

أَمْثِلَةExamples

لِ liye ← لِي mere liye
إِلَى taraf ← إِلَيَّ meri taraf
